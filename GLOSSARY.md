# GTIP Learning System

A platform that teaches people who classify goods for a living how to find the correct GTİP for a good, and how to justify it, by applying the interpretive rules and the section and chapter notes of the Turkish customs tariff.

## Language

### People

**Learner** (Öğrenen):
A person who classifies goods as part of their job, such as a customs broker's assistant or an import/export employee, using the platform to build that skill.
_Avoid_: Student, user

**Content Owner** (İçerik Sorumlusu):
The single domain expert who writes or approves every piece of content before Learners can see it.
_Avoid_: Instructor, admin, teacher

### Content

**Draft** (Taslak):
Content that the Content Owner has not yet approved, whoever or whatever produced it. Learners never see a Draft.

**Published** (Yayında):
Content the Content Owner has approved for Learners.

### Tariff

**Classification** (Sınıflandırma):
Determining the GTİP of a described good by applying the General Interpretive Rules and the relevant section and chapter notes, together with the reasoning that leads to it.
_Avoid_: Lookup, code search

**GTİP** (Gümrük Tarife İstatistik Pozisyonu):
The 12-digit code that identifies a good in the Turkish customs tariff. Digits 1-6 are the international Harmonized System subheading, digits 7-8 the EU Combined Nomenclature subdivision, and digits 9-12 the Turkish national subdivision.
_Avoid_: Tariff code, HS code (an HS code is only the first 6 digits)

**General Interpretive Rules** (Genel Yorum Kuralları):
The six rules of the Harmonized System that govern how any good is classified.
_Avoid_: Classification rules
