# GTIP Learning System

A platform that teaches people who classify goods for a living how to find the correct GTİP for a good, and how to justify it, by applying the interpretive rules and the section and chapter notes of the Turkish customs tariff.

## Language

### People

**Learner** (Öğrenen):
A person who classifies goods as part of their job, such as a customs broker's assistant or an import/export employee, using the platform to build that skill.
_Avoid_: Student, user

**Content Owner** (İçerik Sorumlusu):
The single domain expert who writes or approves every piece of content before Learners can see it.
_Avoid_: Instructor, admin, teacher

### Content

**Draft** (Taslak):
Content that the Content Owner has not yet approved, whoever or whatever produced it. Learners never see a Draft.

**Published** (Yayında):
Content the Content Owner has approved for Learners.

**Exercise** (Alıştırma):
A described good that a Learner classifies Step by Step down to its GTİP, with feedback at every Step.
_Avoid_: Quiz, question, test

**Step** (Adım):
One level of an Exercise: choosing the Section, Chapter, Heading, Subheading or GTİP, together with the rule or note that justifies the choice.

### Tariff

**Classification** (Sınıflandırma):
Determining the GTİP of a described good by applying the General Interpretive Rules and the relevant Section and Chapter Notes, together with the reasoning that leads to it.
_Avoid_: Lookup, code search

**Section** (Bölüm):
One of the 21 groups of Chapters in the tariff, numbered I to XXI.

**Chapter** (Fasıl):
One of the two-digit divisions of the tariff, numbered 01 to 97 (Chapter 77 is reserved).

**Heading** (Pozisyon):
The four-digit level of the tariff, such as 84.71.
_Avoid_: Using "pozisyon" for the full 12-digit code; that is always a GTİP

**Subheading** (Alt Pozisyon):
A subdivision of a Heading, at the six-digit Harmonized System level or the eight-digit Combined Nomenclature level.

**National Subdivision** (Ulusal Kırılım):
Digits 9 to 12 of a GTİP, defined by Turkey.

**GTİP** (Gümrük Tarife İstatistik Pozisyonu):
The 12-digit code that identifies a good in the Turkish customs tariff. Digits 1-6 are the international Harmonized System subheading, digits 7-8 the EU Combined Nomenclature subdivision, and digits 9-12 the National Subdivision.
_Avoid_: Tariff code, HS code (an HS code is only the first 6 digits), pozisyon

**General Interpretive Rules** (Genel Yorum Kuralları, GYK):
The six rules of the Harmonized System that govern how any good is classified.
_Avoid_: Classification rules, GİK

**Section and Chapter Notes** (Bölüm ve Fasıl Notları):
The legally binding notes at the start of a Section or Chapter that define its scope, exclusions and terms.

**Explanatory Notes** (İzahname):
The official commentary on the scope of each Heading and Subheading, published in Turkish as the Gümrük Tarife Cetveli İzahnamesi.

**Binding Tariff Information** (Bağlayıcı Tarife Bilgisi, BTB):
A decision by the Turkish customs administration classifying one specific good, valid for six years.
_Avoid_: Ruling, tarife kararı
